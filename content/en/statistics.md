# Guides

**How do you get the best item in No Man's Sky?** Know where to look. These guides walk the path from the largest thing to the smallest: which [galaxy](/guides/galaxies), which [system](/guides/systems), which planet, and then what the [multitools](/guides/multitool-types), [ships](/guides/ship-types), and [creatures](/guides/creature-roles) there can be. On the way they say which [space station](/guides/space-stations) sells the best upgrade modules, and how to get a class S [capital freighter](/guides/freighters)<!-- living --> or a [living ship](/guides/living-ships)<!-- /living -->.

The numbers come from one walk through [Euclid](/galaxy/b7e59a53-13de-521b-af6b-dcbf6280eb9d) and one galaxy of each other type, from the stars down to what stands on the ground. They change as the count grows.

What the guides lead to is in the catalog: [multitools with adjacent supercharged slots](/tool), [crashed ships](/ship), and [all-S creatures](/creature), with the [star systems](/system) and [planets](/planet) that hold them. Found one already? [How lucky am I?](/how-lucky-am-i) scores it.
