# Living frigates

**What shapes can a living frigate have?** This page puts a cosmic whale together from its parts. Every shape here is one the game makes. Searching for a system is not built yet.

<!-- widget:finder -->

## How to use it

Click a part of the whale, or a name under it, to see its choices; each tile draws its part. A part you leave open can be anything. Every pick is a whale the game builds, so no choice is greyed out. The whale is drawn in plain grey, because its colours come from textures that are not on this page.

A living frigate has one body, and on it sit five parts, each one of a few shapes: the front accessory (one choice is none), the tail, the wings, the side detail, and the front wings. The tendrils trailing behind are part of the tail, and one tail is made of spores, which this page does not draw.

## Getting a living frigate

You call the whale with a Dream Aerial. [Organic frigates](/guides/organic-frigates) explains the blueprint and what each answer of the whale's dream gives.
