# Freighter finder

**What shapes can a regular freighter have?** This freighter builder puts one together from its parts. Every shape here is one the game makes, and you can meet it in any system, so it gives you a shape, not a system.

<!-- widget:finder -->

## How to use it

Pick the hull first. Click a part of the ship, or a name under it, to see its choices; a part you leave open can be anything. A choice the game never makes together with your other picks is greyed out. The ship is drawn in plain grey, because the colours are not part of the shape.

A regular freighter has a box hull, with stacked cargo sections and an upper or lower row of single sections behind them, or a spine hull, a long tube of sections that ends in a bridge, a disc, a sphere, or a ring. Every section of a spine carries one kind of container: twin tanks, crates, spheres, or a covered hangar. The containers of a box hull are discs, boxes, or cylinders, picked apart for the lower and upper halves of its sections, for its row, and for its end. A box hull always shows at least two sections, because its shortest form closes the first one with an end section that carries containers too. The length also sets the size: a regular freighter is small, except a box hull of five or six sections and a spine of five, which are medium.

## Getting a regular freighter

A regular freighter is bought from a fleet. Fleets warp into every kind of system, so you can look for your shape anywhere: look over each fleet that arrives until you see it. The same fleets sell [frigates](/guides/frigates), and each new fleet brings new ones.

## Shape and class

The class is rolled again each time a fleet arrives, so the shape tells you nothing about the class. How often a [regular freighter](/guides/freighters) comes at each class depends on the wealth of the system. The [capital freighter finder](/capital-freighter-finder) gives a system by the shape of the freighter its space battle saves, and the [Interceptor finder](/interceptor-finder) by the shape of its crashed Sentinel ship.
