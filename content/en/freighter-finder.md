# Regular freighters

**What shapes can a regular freighter have?** This page puts a regular freighter together from its parts. Searching for a system is not built yet. Every shape here is one the game makes: the game takes one part from every list on its own, so no pick is a ship that cannot exist.

## How to use it

Pick the hull first. Click a part of the ship, or a name under it, to see its choices; each tile draws its part. A freighter's colours are its system's, not its hull's, so they are not part of the shape: pick a hull and a trim colour from the game's own palette to see the ship in them, or leave them open and it stays grey.

A regular freighter has a box hull, with stacked cargo sections and an upper or lower row of single sections behind them, or a spine hull, a long tube of sections that ends in a bridge, a disc, a sphere, or a ring. A ship shows one kind of container in every section of one place, the first the game picks: twin tanks, crates, spheres, or a covered hangar on a spine, and discs, boxes, or cylinders on a box hull, picked apart for the lower and upper halves of its sections, for its row, and for its end. A box hull always shows at least two sections, because its shortest form closes the first one with an end section that carries containers too. The length also sets the size: a regular freighter is small, except a box hull of five or six sections and a spine of five, which are medium.

## Getting a regular freighter

A regular freighter is bought from a fleet. Fleets warp into every kind of system, so you can look for your shape anywhere: look over each fleet that arrives until you see it. The same fleets sell [frigates](/guides/frigates), and each new fleet brings new ones.

## Shape and class

The class is rolled again each time a fleet arrives, so the shape tells you nothing about the class. How often a [regular freighter](/guides/freighters) comes at each class depends on the wealth of the system. the [capital freighters](/capital-freighter-finder) page shows the shapes of the freighters a space battle saves, and the [Sentinel Interceptors](/interceptor-finder) page the shapes of crashed Sentinel ships.
