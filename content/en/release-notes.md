# Release notes

## 11 November 2026: version 2.3

- Added [quests](/quests): from [The Artemis Path](/quests/the-artemis-path) to [Lost in Space](/quests/lost-in-space), each with its steps, their hints, and every item it gives, and [puzzle answers](/quests/puzzles) for the terminals and monoliths.
- Added a guide to every merchant with a stock: the [Galactic Trade Terminal](/guides/galactic-trade-terminal), the [Cartographer](/guides/cartographer), the [Salvage Exchange](/guides/salvage-exchange), the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer), the [Forsaken Trader](/guides/forsaken-trader), the [Outlaw Station](/guides/outlaw-station), starship pilots and alien traders, and more. Each lists everything it sells, with pictures and prices, and a barter merchant shows how likely it is to take your offer.
- The fossils moved to the [Fossil Collector](/guides/fossil-collector), who trades them.
- Added the [corvettes](/guides/corvettes) and [corvette modules](/guides/corvette-modules) guides: how to build a corvette, where its parts come from, and what each part adds.
- Smaller corrections across the guides: illegal modules come from an Outlaw Station and the Scrap Dealer, an upgraded ship's stats are rolled again at its new class, and every guide now links the merchants and quests it names.

## 8 November 2026: version 2.2

- Added [fishing](/guides/fishing): every fish, where and when it bites, what bait does, and why a fish is worth catching.
- Added [excavation finds](/guides/excavation-finds): every fossil you dig up at Ancient Bones, and the skeleton displays they build.
- Added [battle moves](/guides/battle-moves): every move of a companion's five slots, how gene edits and Retroviral Pellets reroll them, the Arena League, the medal eggs, and the eggs your own companions lay.
- [Frigates](/guides/frigates) now hold every trait, what a stat brings home from an expedition, damage and loss, and how a frigate levels up.
- Added [game bugs](/game-bugs): where the game says one thing and does another.
- Fishing and excavation finds sit with the planets, and the guides read in one line from the galaxy down to the creatures.
- Smaller corrections across the guides: a freighter battle counts the moment it starts, a dreadnought's module stays yours when the freighter you saved is lost, and a module's stats are rolled when you install it.

## 6 November 2026: version 2.1

- Added the modules of every item: what each upgrade module can raise at each class, and how often an install rolls each stat. They are on [multitool modules](/guides/multitool-modules), [starship modules](/guides/starship-modules), [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules), [freighter technology](/guides/freighter-technology), the modules of each exocraft from the [Roamer](/guides/roamer-modules) to the [Minotaur](/guides/minotaur-modules), and [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots), which says where each module goes.
- [How lucky am I?](/how-lucky-am-i) now weighs supercharged slots by what their figure is worth in damage: a square above every other figure, and a T below.
