# Weapons Research

**What does the quest Weapons Research give?** Weapon blueprints: the Positron Ejector, the Infra-Knife Accelerator, and the Cyclotron Ballista for your starship, and the Advanced Mining Laser for your multitool. They come in the last of its {{parts}} parts, each one you do not know yet; on the way the armourer also hands over a Starshield Battery and Unstable Plasma.

## How it starts

The quest opens once you have hired an armourer at the Weapons Terminal, which [Expanding the Base](/quests/expanding-the-base) gives.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends: you shut down a weapons factory, hunt and interrogate pirates, raid depots, collect Vy'keen daggers, and fight the Sentinels, returning to the armourer each time. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
