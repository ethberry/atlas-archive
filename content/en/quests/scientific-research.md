# Scientific Research

**What does the quest Scientific Research give?** The base scientist's blueprints across its {{parts}} parts: the Large Refiner, the Autonomous Mining Unit and the Atmosphere Harvester, the Save Beacon, and the products the scientist teaches you to craft, from Acid and Lubricant to the Circuit Board.

## How it starts

The quest opens once you have hired a scientist at the Science Terminal, which [Expanding the Base](/quests/expanding-the-base) gives.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
