# The Atlas Path

**What does the Atlas Path give?** At its last Atlas Interface you choose: birth a new star and receive the Star Seed, or walk away and receive the Mark of the Denier, both exosuit modules. The way there is {{parts}} parts, from one Atlas Station to the next.

## How it starts

The path opens after The Space Anomaly, the first mission aboard the Anomaly: its first step is to locate an Atlas Interface.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends: locate the next Atlas Station, explore the universe, and return to its interface, station after station. The last part is Atlas Eternal, which asks for a wonder to show the Atlas after you birth a star. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it; the choice at the last interface is one row, its options as A and B, each with what it gives.
