# Dreams of the Deep

**What does the quest Dreams of the Deep give?** The Nautilon Chamber, where you build the Nautilon submarine, the blueprint of its High-Power Sonar, and the parts of an underwater base; across its {{parts}} parts it ends with the Lost Bathysphere, an Exosuit Appearance Override.

## How it starts

The quest opens after you finish the first mission of The Purge, in the story of [The Artemis Path](/quests/the-artemis-path): your starship picks up a decayed signal asking for help nearby.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
