# Puzzle answers

**What is the right answer to a puzzle at a terminal?** Find what the terminal shows below: the game has {{sequences}} number puzzles and {{questions}} questions, each with its right answer.

## How a puzzle works

A building's terminal, or a monolith, shows its text and offers answers to pick from. A right answer pays what the building gives; a wrong one pays nothing, or costs you standing or health. What you get depends on the building.

## Number puzzles

The {{sequenceBuildings}} show a row of numbers with stars in place of the next one. Look up your numbers in the table, which runs by the first number, then the next.

## Questions

The {{questionBuildings}} ask a question in words: an alarm to stop, a machine to fix, or a vision to answer. The table runs by the text the building shows first. Where more than one answer is right, each stands in the row; where the right answer opens a second step, its answer follows.

A monolith also holds a multitool, always an Atlantid one, as [multitool types](/guides/multitool-types) shows.
