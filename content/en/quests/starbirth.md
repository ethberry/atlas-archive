# Starbirth

**What does the quest Starbirth give?** A living ship, and on the way the Solar Ray and the Animus Beam for your multitool. Across its {{parts}} parts you follow the call of your Void Egg, grow the ship's parts, and make contact.

## How it starts

The quest begins once you carry a Void Egg, which the Quicksilver merchant of the Space Anomaly sells.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The last part sends you to find the new lifeform: the living ship itself. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
