# In Stellar Multitudes

**What does the quest In Stellar Multitudes give?** The Atlantid Drive for your starship and the Resonance Matrix for your freighter: the drives that pass the dissonant space around purple stars. Across its {{parts}} parts it also hands over the Memories of Atlantid, a Reality Anchor, and the Anomalous Scaffold.

## How it starts

The quest opens while you fly your starship in space, once you have finished [The Atlas Path](/quests/the-atlas-path) and [They Who Returned](/quests/they-who-returned), the quest of the Autophage.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way, side conversations included.

## The other way to the drives

The Atlantid Drive is also a blueprint of the Space Anomaly, and the Resonance Matrix a Capital Ship Research blueprint. Where the drives stand among the other modules is in the [starship modules](/guides/starship-modules) and [freighter technology](/guides/freighter-technology) guides.
