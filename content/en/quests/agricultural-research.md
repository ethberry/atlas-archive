# Agricultural Research

**What does the quest Agricultural Research give?** The seeds of the base farm's crops and the Hydroponic Trays to grow them in: the base farmer hands over a crop, asks you to grow and harvest it, and gives the next, from the Gutrot Flower to the Mordite Root, across its {{parts}} parts.

## How it starts

The quest opens once you have hired a farmer at the Agricultural Terminal, which [Expanding the Base](/quests/expanding-the-base) gives.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. Each asks you to grow and harvest the crop the part before it gave. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.

## What the items do

Everything the quest hands over, with the description the game gives it: the seeds, and the products the farmer teaches you to make from the harvest.
