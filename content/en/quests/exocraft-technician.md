# Exocraft Technician

**What does the quest Exocraft Technician give?** The blueprints of the land exocraft's equipment: the Exocraft Radar with its Radar Amplifier and Radar Power Resonator, the Mounted Cannon, the Exocraft Boosters, and both mining lasers, with the Geobays of the Roamer, the Nomad, and the Colossus, across its {{parts}} parts.

## How it starts

The quest opens at your base once you have finished [Expanding the Base](/quests/expanding-the-base), the overseer's quest: build an Exocraft Terminal there and hire an Exocraft Technician.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. Almost every part sends you back to the technician, who hands over the next blueprint. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
