# The Artemis Path

**What does the Artemis Path give?** Across its {{parts}} parts: Artemis' Translator and the Personal Forcefield early on, the first parts of your base with its Base Computer and Roamer Geobay, the Mind Ark and the Soul Engine in The First Traveller, and Remembrance, an exosuit module, once The Purge is done.

## How it starts

The story starts with the game: Awakenings sends you to a stranger's coordinates, and each chapter follows the one before, from Alone Amidst the Stars and Ghosts in the Machine to A Leap in the Dark, The First Traveller, Patterns in Time, 16 / 16, and The Purge.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
