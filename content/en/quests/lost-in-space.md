# Lost in Space

**What does the quest Lost in Space give?** No item of its own. It is the derelict freighter's walk: land on it, explore it, and get back to your starship, then find a buyer for what you salvaged at a space station. What a derelict gives lies in its containers and at its engineering control panel, which the [freighter technology](/guides/freighter-technology) guide covers.

## How it starts

The quest opens when a derelict freighter appears near you, a ghostly freighter lost in deep space; the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer) of a space station sells the place of one. Its last part opens once you hold the derelict's salvage aboard a space station.
