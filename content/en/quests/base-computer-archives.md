# Base Computer Archives

**What do the Base Computer Archives give?** Blueprints for your base, your starship, and your exosuit: the Teleport Receiver and the Efficient Thrusters for the starship, the Neural Stimulator and the Shield Lattice for the exosuit, and base parts from the Storage Container and the Solar Panel to the Landing Pad. Of its {{parts}} parts, the last ones restore off-world archives: lore, with no item.

## How it starts

The archives open early in [The Artemis Path](/quests/the-artemis-path), after Alone Amidst the Stars, in the Base Computer of your base. Each early part gives a blueprint, asks you to build or install it, and then to know more words of the alien languages before the next archive can be read.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. A word step counts every word you know, so the number after it is the total you need, not the words to learn in that part. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
