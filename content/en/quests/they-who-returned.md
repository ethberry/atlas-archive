# They Who Returned

**What does the quest They Who Returned give?** The blueprints of a Voltaic Staff: in its part Audience with the Autophage, the Autophage hand over the Fibreglass Grip, the Atlantideum Chamber, and the Concentric Transducer. Across its {{parts}} parts it also gives the Scan Harmoniser and the Polyphonic Core for your multitool's scanner, and Atlantideum.

## How it starts

The quest begins with a navigational hijack: your starship is pulled toward remote coordinates, and the source is an abandoned camp where a disembodied Autophage head waits.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log. After the Polyphonic Core, the quest opens Audience with the Autophage, which gives the staff, and Prayers of the Machine, which gives Atlantideum, side by side and waits for both; then the last part takes the staff to a hidden gateway. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
