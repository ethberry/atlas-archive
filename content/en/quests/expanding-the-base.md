# Expanding the Base

**What does the quest Expanding the Base give?** The terminals that bring specialists to your base: the Construction Terminal for the overseer, then the Science, Weapons, Agricultural, Galactic Trade, and Exocraft Terminals, across its {{parts}} parts. Most of them open another quest of the base.

## How it starts

The overseer's quest opens at a base you have claimed, early in [The Artemis Path](/quests/the-artemis-path). It is the first of the base quests: the Science Terminal it gives opens [Scientific Research](/quests/scientific-research), the Weapons Terminal [Weapons Research](/quests/weapons-research), the Agricultural Terminal [Agricultural Research](/quests/agricultural-research), and its last part the [Exocraft Technician](/quests/exocraft-technician).

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The overseer asks for a material or a task each time, and most parts hand over a terminal. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.
