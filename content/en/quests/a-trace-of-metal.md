# A Trace of Metal

**What does the quest A Trace of Metal give?** Across its {{parts}} parts it gives the blueprints of the Hardframe Right Arm, Left Arm, Legs, and Body for the Minotaur, of the Minotaur AI Pilot, and of the Paralysis Mortar.

## How it starts

The quest begins at your settlement: the first step answers your settlers' call, and the next defends the settlement from a Sentinel assault.

## The steps

Each part is one entry of the log, and the next opens when it ends. The table gives every step as the log names it, with the hint the game shows under it, and on a part's last step everything that part gives along the way.

## What the items do

Everything the quest hands over, with the description the game gives it. The quest takes some of them back: the Nanite Filament, Portal Phylactery, Beads of Glass, and Undamaged Drone Shell go into the drone, and the Pristine Brain is traded for the Hacked Brain.

<!-- widget:items -->

The first part rebuilds a Sentinel drone, The Sentry, from the parts the game hands over on the way, and The Sentry leads the rest of the quest. The last part asks for a pristine Walker Brain: Iteration Tethys prepares it as the Hacked Brain, an Atlas Beacon cuts it from the Sentinel network, and the Hardframe Body it goes into lets the Minotaur fight on its own.

## The other way to the blueprints

The Hardframe parts and the Minotaur AI Pilot also stand in the Minotaur tree of the Space Anomaly, and the Paralysis Mortar in its weapon tree, sold for nanites. What each Hardframe part adds to the Minotaur is in the [Minotaur modules](/guides/minotaur-modules) guide.
