# Game bugs

**Which bugs of No Man's Sky change what you farm?** The ones where a card or a table of the game says one thing and the game does another, so you look in the wrong place or build for a stat you do not get. Each entry says what the game shows and what it does instead.

## Sentinels on a gas giant

A gas giant's planet card can read High Sentinel Activity while no Sentinel comes on the ground: mining in the open at a crashed freighter on such a gas giant, the wanted level stayed at zero. On a gas giant, the card's Sentinel line does not tell you what you will meet. How often each activity comes on each kind of world is on [Sentinels and dissonance](/guides/sentinels-and-dissonance).

## The ship code differs between PC and Mac

The same ship can carry a different name on another platform. Most ship names end or begin with a code of two letters and a digit, such as `GH4`, and the PC build draws the three parts of that code in the opposite order to the Mac build. So a ship that reads `Tosek GH4` on a Mac reads `Tosek KH3` on a PC; the words are the same, and so is the ship. The PlayStation 4 shows the Mac order, and the Xbox shows the PC order. The site prints the PC order, so on a Mac or a PlayStation 4 the code you see in the game can differ from the one in a ship's name here.

## The Speed organic frigate

The first cosmic whale asks you questions in a dream, and the count your answers raise most picks the kind of your first organic frigate. The game's table points the Speed answers at the Support count, so the Speed count never grows and the dream cannot give a Speed frigate. Later whales still sell Speed frigates. Whether any dream at all gives one has not been researched yet in the game; the dream's answers are on [organic frigates](/guides/organic-frigates).
