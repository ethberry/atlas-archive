# Starship Pilot

**What do starship pilots sell?** Trade goods, fuel, charts, and frigate modules. A pilot shows {{shownMin}} items, and the list depends on the pilot's race: {{wares}} for a Gek, {{wares2}} for a Vy'keen, {{wares3}} for a Korvax.

## Where to find one

Pilots land on a space station, on the pads of a trading post or a Colossal Archive, or out in the open on a planet; talk to one at their ship and choose to trade. The same pilot also sells the ship itself, as [ship types](/guides/ship-types) tells.
