# Interceptor prices

**How much is a Sentinel Interceptor worth?** As much as its cargo slots say: from {{lowC}} million units with {{fewest}} slots to {{highC}} million with {{most}} at class C, and up to {{highS}} million at class S.

## Slots set the price

The game prices a ship by its cargo grid, not by its looks or its weapons. Each slot adds more than the one before, so a ship with {{most}} slots is worth about {{times}} times one with {{fewest}}. The class adds a share on top: {{addB}} at B, {{addA}} at A, and {{addS}} at S. Count the cargo slots on the claim screen and read the row.
