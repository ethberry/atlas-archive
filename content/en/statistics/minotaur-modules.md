# Minotaur modules

**What can a Minotaur upgrade module raise?** Its laser, flamethrower, cannon, and Daedalus Engine each have a family, and none has a C-Class module: they run from B to S.

Each stat reads as the module's card prints it. The land exocraft share a set of their own, with a page each: the [Roamer](/guides/roamer-modules), [Nomad](/guides/nomad-modules), [Pilgrim](/guides/pilgrim-modules), and [Colossus](/guides/colossus-modules).

## Every Minotaur module

The modules you build or receive, apart from upgrade modules. The Hardframe body, arms, and legs are a blueprint of the Space Anomaly, and the quest [A Trace of Metal](/quests/a-trace-of-metal) gives them too. The Liquidator set, whose right arm carries a flamethrower, is a reward of the Liquidators expedition: its milestones give the body, both arms, and the legs, and the Quicksilver merchant of the Space Anomaly sells them too. The Minotaur wears one set at a time.

## The two body sets

The Minotaur wears one set at a time: a body, a left arm, a right arm, and legs. The Hardframe set is blue and the Liquidator set white. Each part below shows the stats the game gives it: as its card prints them where the part is an upgrade (the left arm of the Hardframe and both pairs of legs), otherwise as the game stores them, since the cards of the other arms and of the bodies print no stats.

The two sets match part for part but for the arms: the Hardframe left arm adds to the mining laser and sets targets on fire, and its right arm stuns, while the Liquidator left arm stuns and its right arm carries the flamethrower. The bodies both switch on the Minotaur AI Pilot, and the legs give the same fuel and boost.

## Supercharged slots

The Minotaur has three supercharged slots, and they stand in the same places for every player: slot 8 of the top row, slot 1 of the middle row, slot 5 of the bottom row, counting from the left. There is nothing to roll. None of the three is adjacent to another, so each can lift one kind: give them to the Daedalus Engine, the Minotaur Laser, and the Minotaur Cannon, one module of each, and set the rest of that kind and its own technology around it, adjacent.
