# Ship types

**Which ship types lie in wrecks?** Seven ship types, mostly four of them. Shuttles are {{shuttle}} of them, Haulers {{hauler}}, Fighters {{fighter}}, and Explorers {{explorer}}. Solars are {{solar}} and Interceptors {{interceptor}}. An Exotic is {{exoticOneIn}}.

A wreck is not the only way. You can also buy a ship from its pilot wherever it lands: on a space station, on the pads of a trading post or a Colossal Archive, or out in the open on a planet. Pilots do not land at a shop.

What each ship type does best is under [ship stats](/guides/ship-stats).

## By source

Crashed ships stand on {{crashedPlanets}} of planets. Sunken ones need a sea and reach {{sunkenPlanets}}. Both pay the same mix of ships. Sentinel wrecks stand on dissonant planets only, {{sentinelPlanets}} of all planets, and every one is an [Interceptor](/guides/interceptors). No other source gives one.

## By economy

[Wealth](/guides/economy) moves one ship type. A pirate system turns most Shuttles into Solars: {{pirateSolar}} of its wrecks, against {{lowSolar}} at low wealth. Wealth matters far more for [ship classes](/guides/ship-classes).

The economy type changes nothing: across the seven types no ship type's share moves by more than {{economySwing}} points.

## By race

Each of the [races](/guides/races) favours one ship type, about twice as much as the next: Gek systems hold {{gekHauler}} Haulers, Vy'keen {{vykeenFighter}} Fighters, Korvax {{korvaxExplorer}} Explorers. Uncharted systems spread evenly, and hold no Interceptor.

<!-- widget:races -->

A ship's type also decides how likely it is to be class S, since one roll sets both: [ship classes](/guides/ship-classes).
