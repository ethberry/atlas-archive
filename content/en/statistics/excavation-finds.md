# Excavation finds

**What do you dig up at Ancient Bones in No Man's Sky?** Fossils. A planet that shows Ancient Bones holds bone piles of four kinds, and each pile gives one fossil of its own kind: a skull, limbs, a torso, or a tail. The game has {{fossils}} fossils, and bone piles give {{dug}} of them; the tables below list all {{fossils}}.

## What bones are for

A bone is money or decor. Each fossil's card says {{boneValue}} units. Or build it into a skeleton: the game has {{displays}} skeleton displays for a base, each with {{displaySlots}} slots, one bone in each, and any limb fits any limb or wing slot. A single bone can also stand on its own in a Fossil Display Case.

<!-- widget:displays -->

A finished skeleton can be packaged into one exhibit, which the game says can then be sold, traded, or placed again at any base. Hello Games' notes for the update that added fossils say the Fossil Collectors on space stations buy exhibits and trade fossils for your spare bones; what they pay is not researched yet.
