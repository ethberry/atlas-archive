# Tetris figures

**Which figure will a multitool's four supercharged slots form?** Most often a corner, when they are adjacent at all. [How it works](/about) explains why that matters, and [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots) how much each figure adds to damage and where the weapon goes on it. A staff and a tool do not land on the same figures, and turns of one figure do not come out equal: a corner can be {{cornerGap}} times rarer than the same corner turned.

## All 19, by type

Figures run by how many ways they can lie: the square one way, the line two, each zigzag two, the T four, and each corner four. Inside a figure, the rarest turn comes first.

Most types land about alike. Experimental and Alien land alike too, and apart from the rest: a rare body needs a low first roll, and that same roll places a slot at the left edge of the grid. The colour of an Atlantid works the same way, which is why its three columns differ.

Staffs fill the All tools column, because there are so many more of them. Read a type's own column instead. A flat line is rare on a staff, {{staffLine}}, and ordinary on a rifle, {{rifleLine}}. A tool's slots come from two separate rolls, so any figure can turn up. A staff fills its slots from fixed places, so its figure is largely set from the start, as [staffs](/guides/staffs) shows.

The figure everyone wants is rarer than it looks: [the square](/guides/square).
