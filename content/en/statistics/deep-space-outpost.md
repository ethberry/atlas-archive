# Deep-Space Outpost

**What does a Deep-Space Outpost sell?** Charts to deep space, the salvage of hulks, and contraband. Its merchant shows {{shownMin}} items from a list of {{wares}}: the Habitable Asteroid Chart and the Salvage Claim always, the rest drawn.

## What it sells

Every item it can show, with the description the game gives it and its value. A Habitable Asteroid Chart marks an asteroid where a base can be built; a Salvage Claim marks a large wreck rich with salvage.

At the edges of a deep-space outpost you can summon the [Forsaken Trader](/guides/forsaken-trader), who trades biomechanical corvette parts for Haunting Mementos.
