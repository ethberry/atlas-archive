# Sources by planet size

**How much does a planet's size give you?** A lot: a {{highName}} planet holds a median of {{high}} spots, a {{lowName}} {{low}}: about {{ratio}} times fewer. Size scales every source alike, so the mix of sources stays the same.

A spot is a place on a planet's grid that holds a building; [where a multitool comes from](/about#where-a-multitool-comes-from) lists the kinds. Spots count above sea level, and sunken ships are the exception: they stand deep under the water. Uncharted systems are in these medians.
