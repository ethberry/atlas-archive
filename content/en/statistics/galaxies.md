# Galaxies

**Which galaxy is best to search?** The one you are in, unless you hunt [Sentinel tools](/guides/multitool-types) or [Interceptors](/guides/interceptors): a Harsh galaxy nearly doubles both. Otherwise the type of a galaxy changes the planets and the Sentinels, and little else.

## What changes in another galaxy

A Lush galaxy trades Dead worlds and the common rocky types for Lush worlds, so fewer planets stand without a creature. An Empty galaxy goes the other way, with far fewer Lush worlds, more Weird ones, and a little less water. A Harsh galaxy keeps the planets of [Euclid](/galaxy/b7e59a53-13de-521b-af6b-dcbf6280eb9d) and changes the Sentinels: nearly three times as many aggressive planets, and nearly twice the dissonance, which is what doubles those tools and ships. Lush and Empty galaxies both hold more Red, Green, and Blue worlds.

## The ring

Of 256 galaxies, {{normal}} are Normal, {{lush}} Lush, {{harsh}} Harsh, and {{empty}} Empty. A jump through the galaxy core takes you to the next galaxy, so the second galaxy is one jump from Euclid and the tenth is nine. The types repeat around a ring, so the nearest of each lies close: [Calypso](/galaxy/8b530a0e-fdc9-50a6-969b-6ea1773fa26f) is Harsh, two jumps away, [Budullangr](/galaxy/f0913c73-fbc9-50b0-86a2-b405a0c56a11) Empty at six, and [Eissentam](/galaxy/1f9bc2b3-5d1e-5972-8415-954b2acef14a) Lush at nine.
