# Star systems

**What does a star system hold?** Up to six bodies and, most of the time, a space station. {{six}} of systems hold the full six bodies, and only {{two}} stop at two. {{noMoons}} have no moon. A gas giant turns up in {{giants}} of systems, and only at purple stars.

## Star colours

Yellow rules. Purple stars are the one colour with a median of one moon, and the only home of gas giants.

## Sizes and types
