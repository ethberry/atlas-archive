# Corvette modules

**What can a corvette upgrade module raise?** A corvette has four families of its own, flight control, navigation, defensive, and cargo systems, from C to S. It also takes starship upgrade modules made for it, at B and A only. A derelict freighter's engineering control panel can Fabricate Corvette Modules too, but that gives corvette parts to build with, not upgrade modules: always two or three of one hull part (plating, wings, joints, or a decoration), and often one working part as well (a cockpit, hab, engine, thruster, reactor, weapon, landing gear, or shield).

Each stat reads as the module's card prints it. Below each table a second one shows what one install rolls: how many stats it raises, how often it comes near the best its kind can add to the stat it names, and how often each stat is raised and lands in the top tenth of its limits, where the Illegal modules stand out by a one-in-ten draw that raises the most stats their kind allows.
