# Space stations

**What does a space station sell?** An inhabited station has {{merchants}} technology merchants, one each for starship, multitool, exosuit, and exocraft modules. What each of them sells belongs to that station: the same items every visit, whatever the time. A pirate station sells suspicious modules instead, and an abandoned station sells no technology, though you can still [upgrade a multitool](/guides/multitool-upgrades) there.

## Three kinds of station

<!-- widget:kinds -->

Most systems hold an inhabited station. How often each kind turns up is under [star systems](/guides/systems), and an Uncharted system has no station at all.

The multitool cabinets beside the shops pay the same mix of tools as a shop on a planet, as [multitool types](/guides/multitool-types) explains.

## The merchants

<!-- widget:merchants -->

Every merchant has a list of what it can sell and a list of what it always sells. The stock is the always list plus an even pick from the rest, and the size of the stock is a roll of its own. The starship merchant always sells the four reactors, class C to S, and the class C Launch Thrusters, Pulse Engine, Hyperdrive, and Starship Shield modules. Everything else is a pick.

The pick is the station's own. It does not change with time, with how often you visit, or with a reload. To see other modules, fly to another system.

## The other merchants

Beside the technology merchants, a station trades goods at its [Galactic Trade Terminal](/guides/galactic-trade-terminal) and charts at its [Cartographer](/guides/cartographer). The [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer) sells for Tainted Metal, the [Salvage Exchange](/guides/salvage-exchange) trades corvette parts, and the [Fossil Collector](/guides/fossil-collector) trades fossils. Pilots who land in the hangar trade from their ships as a [Starship Pilot](/guides/starship-pilots), and any other alien you can talk to trades as an [Alien Trader](/guides/alien-traders). Away from a station, a settlement's Marketplace has a [Settlement Technology Merchant](/guides/settlement-technology-merchant), and the Space Anomaly a [Space Anomaly Module Merchant](/guides/anomaly-module-merchant).

## What does not change the stock

The system's wealth, economy type, conflict level, and race change nothing a technology merchant sells. A wealthy system and a poor one draw from the same list with the same odds. Only a pirate system is different, because its station is a different station.

A pirate station's technology merchant shows {{pirateShown}} of {{pirateAll}} suspicious modules, and its black market sells Starshield Batteries and Suspicious Packets, as [Outlaw Station](/guides/outlaw-station) lists.

The odds of each module at each class, and how rare a station that sells everything at S is, are on [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants).
