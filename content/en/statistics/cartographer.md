# Cartographer

**What does the Cartographer sell?** Charts for Navigation Data: {{wares}} kinds, all on sale at once, each leading to one kind of site on a nearby planet.

## Where to find one

Every space station has a Cartographer, who gives directions to outposts and other buildings.

## What it sells

Every chart, with the description the game gives it and its price in Navigation Data. Each Planetary Chart marks a different site: a secure site with blueprints, a distress signal, an inhabited outpost, or an ancient relic. At a secure site a terminal asks a question before it opens: the right answers are in [puzzle answers](/quests/puzzles).

A distress signal marks a crashed starship, which you can claim; how to find a good one is under [ship classes](/guides/ship-classes). Navigation Data is on sale at a [Galactic Trade Terminal](/guides/galactic-trade-terminal) off a station, and now and then from a [Starship Pilot](/guides/starship-pilots) or an [Alien Trader](/guides/alien-traders).
