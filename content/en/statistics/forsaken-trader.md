# Forsaken Trader

**What does the Forsaken Trader sell?** Corvette parts of a biomechanical kind, {{wares}} of them, from cockpits and habs to thrusters, wings, and hull pieces, traded for Haunting Mementos; and, for {{offeringAmount}} Preserved Grubs, the Larva Cannon, a class S multitool.

## Where to find one

A Forsaken Trader is summoned at the edge of a [Deep-Space Outpost](/guides/deep-space-outpost). Each has every one of its items on sale.

## What it sells

Every item it can show, with the description the game gives it and the value the trade counts for it. A few cockpits, thrusters, and reactors come from no one else, as [corvettes](/guides/corvettes) shows.

## What it takes

The trader takes the Haunting Mementos, each counted at {{wantedValue}}, and asks {{barter}} times the value of what you take: it sells below value. It rates each memento at {{floor}} to the whole of its value, and an offer has {{slots}} slots, each holding a stack, so the last column of the stock is how many mementos buy each part whatever the rating; fewer may still pass, with a smaller chance. The answer stays the same for the same offer until you next fly into space, so after a refusal fly out and back, or change the offer.
