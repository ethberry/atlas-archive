# Colossus modules

**What can the Colossus carry?** The Colossus, the big hauler, takes every land exocraft module, 30 technology slots of them, and upgrade modules of four kinds from C to S: Mounted Cannon, Exocraft Mining Laser, Exocraft Boosters, and Fusion Engine. It also takes two modules no other exocraft does: the Excavation Blade, which clears the ground ahead, and the Mineral Processing Rig, which refines what it mines.

The Roamer, Nomad, Pilgrim, and Colossus share their upgrade modules and most of their technology; what sets them apart is the room. Each stat reads as the module's card prints it.

## How much fits

With every module on it the Colossus has 10 slots left for upgrade modules: room for three of each of three kinds and one more, so one kind gets a single module. Every core technology takes three upgrade modules at most, and a fourth of the same kind switches all of them off. Where to put them for the most effect is on [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots), and where to buy them on [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants).

## Supercharged slots

The Colossus has three supercharged slots, and they stand in the same places for every player: slot 9 of the top row, slot 8 of the middle row, slot 6 of the bottom row, counting from the left. There is nothing to roll. None of the three touches another, so each can lift one kind: give them to the Fusion Engine, the Exocraft Boosters, and the Exocraft Mining Laser, one module of each, and set the rest of that kind and its own technology around it, touching.

## Upgrade modules

One table per family: each class, how many stats one install raises, and the limits of each stat. Below each table a second one shows what one install rolls: how many stats it raises, how often it comes near the best its kind can add to the stat it names, and how often each stat is raised; a third shows how often a raised stat lands in the top tenth of its limits.
