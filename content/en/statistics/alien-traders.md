# Alien Trader

**What does an alien NPC sell?** Trade goods, fuel, and charts, from a list of its race: {{wares}} items for a Gek, {{wares2}} for a Vy'keen, {{wares3}} for a Korvax. One shows {{shownMin}} at a time.

## Who trades

Any Gek, Vy'keen, or Korvax you can talk to who is not a merchant, a specialist, or a pilot: a traveller in a building on a planet, a visitor of a space station, a survivor at a crash site, or a scavenger. Choose to trade in the talk.
