# Creature grades

**Where do you find an all-S creature?** On any planet. All three grades at S come to {{bestOneIn}} creatures on every type, size, and galaxy, and the only thing a planet changes is how many creatures it lists. {{planetsWith}} of planets hold one such creature. Two on one planet is the real find: {{twoOneIn}} planets. The [creatures to adopt](/creature) come with their grades and the planet and portal glyphs of each.

## The odds

Each grade is rolled apart, with S at 15%. Nothing about the planet enters that roll, so these odds hold everywhere.

## Long lists

More creatures mean more rolls. A one-creature list holds an all-S creature {{oneOne}} of the time, five creatures {{fiveOne}}, and ten {{tenOne}}. The Found line of a new discovery counts fish and birds as well, so a high number is a rough sign, not a promise. [Creature roles](/guides/creature-roles) shows how long lists run.

## By planet type

The type counts only through the length of its list. Lush worlds give {{lushOne}}, Weird worlds {{weirdOne}}, and Waterworlds {{waterOne}}. Dead worlds and gas giants give nothing.

<!-- widget:biomes -->

## By size

Size barely moves the list, and so barely moves the odds. Moons run a little short. Most giants are gas giants, which hold nothing, and that pulls their row down.

<!-- widget:sizes -->

## By galaxy

The type of a galaxy does not change the odds either. Go where you already are.

<!-- widget:galaxies -->

## By affinity

No [creature affinity](/guides/creature-affinity) is favoured: each holds the same part of all-S creatures as of all creatures.

<!-- widget:affinities -->

## Two on one planet

Two all-S creatures on a four-creature list happen about {{fourTwo}} times, and we have seen it. Three on one planet are far rarer still.

One more place to look: a creature of a role seen at another size is a separate species, with its own name and grades. The odds here count each creature at the size of its role.

An S on the card is a number, and not always a bigger one than an A: [creature stats](/guides/creature-stats).
