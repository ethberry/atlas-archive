# How to upgrade a multitool

**Should you upgrade a tool or find one at S?** Find one if damage is what you want, and check the type before you pay. An upgrade rolls a tool's base stats again, and for some types that roll is fixed low: an Alien or Experimental tool upgraded to S always lands at the floor of its damage. A tool found at S keeps its own roll. The numbers of a found tool are under [multitool stats](/guides/multitool-stats).

## Where to upgrade

Any inhabited or abandoned [space station](/guides/space-stations) will do. An abandoned station sells no technology, but it still upgrades a multitool. A pirate station does not offer it.

## What an upgrade changes

The class goes up, the supercharged slots fill towards four, and the base stats come from a roll that belongs to the tool. Once the tool is in your hand that roll is fixed: upgrading from C or from B ends on one result, and you cannot roll again by paying again. To get another result you need another tool.

## Damage after an upgrade

The first roll of a tool also picks its type, so the type limits where an upgrade can land.

An Atlantid's colour tells you its damage before you pay. Rifles, Sentinels, and Royals spread over the whole range, so those are the ones worth hunting for a high result.

## Staffs

Every staff upgraded at a station ends on one and the same base: {{staffDamage}} damage, {{staffMining}} mining, and {{staffScanner}} scanner. Only a staff that is built at class S keeps its own roll, up to 50% damage.

An upgrade fills the empty supercharged slots, and you cannot choose where they land. [Tetris figures](/guides/figures) shows where they fall.
