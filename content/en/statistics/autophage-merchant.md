# Autophage Synthesis Terminal

**What does the Autophage Synthesis Terminal sell?** Autophage outfits for Void Motes: {{wares}} items, all on sale at once, most of them Exosuit Appearance Overrides, with a Repair Kit, the Rebuilt Exosuit Module, a salvaged exosuit upgrade, and a chart to an Autophage settlement.

## Where to find one

The terminal stands in Autophage settlements. Its other option, staff assembly, builds a staff from the parts you bring, as [staffs](/guides/staffs) covers; the quest [They Who Returned](/quests/they-who-returned) gives the blueprints of those parts.

## What it sells

Every item it can show, with the description the game gives it and its price in Void Motes.
