# Space Anomaly Module Merchant

**What does the Space Anomaly's module merchant sell?** Upgrade modules at the lowest class, C and a few at B: {{wares}} kinds for the starship, multitool, exosuit, and exocraft, all of them on sale at once, aboard the Space Anomaly.

## What it sells

Every module it can show, with the description the game gives it and its value. What a module adds is in the guide of its item, from [starship modules](/guides/starship-modules) to [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules); class S ones are sold at a station, as [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants) shows.
