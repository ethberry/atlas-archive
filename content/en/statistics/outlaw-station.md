# Outlaw Station

**What do the merchants of an Outlaw Station sell?** Suspicious modules and contraband. Its technology merchant shows {{shownMin}} of {{wares}} suspicious modules; its black market shows {{shownMin2}} to {{shownMax2}} items from a list of {{wares2}}.

## Where to find one

An Outlaw Station stands in a pirate system, in place of a regular space station.

## The technology merchant

Every suspicious module it can show, with the description the game gives it and its value. A suspicious module installs as an Illegal module, class X; how often each is on sale, and how a set comes together, is on [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants#pirate-stations).

## The black market

Starshield Batteries, Suspicious Packets, Tritium, and Di-hydrogen are always on sale; the rest is drawn.
