# Interceptors

**How do you get a Sentinel Interceptor?** Claim its wreck on a dissonant planet, but not every one holds them: {{planets}} of planets hold them, {{ofDissonant}} of the dissonant ones. A dissonant Waterworld, Dead world, or Weird world holds none, so look on Red, Green, and Blue worlds.

## Which planets

<!-- widget:biomes -->

A planet that holds them holds a median of {{median}} spots. They are {{wrecks}} of all wrecks, and [Sentinels and dissonance](/guides/sentinels-and-dissonance) has the rest of what those planets hold.

## One form in a system

Every Sentinel wreck of a system shows the same ship: the same parts and the same colours. Its name, class, and supercharged slots change from wreck to wreck. So when you want a form, you want a system<!-- interceptor-finder -->, and the [Interceptor builder](/interceptor-finder) shows every form there is<!-- /interceptor-finder -->.

## Wealth and class

[Wealth](/guides/economy) sets the class as it does for every ship: a low wealth system holds no class S Interceptor, a high one {{highS}}, and a pirate one {{pirateS}}. It leaves adjacent slots alone.

## Adjacent slots

{{adjacent}} of Interceptors hold adjacent slots at class S, against {{otherAdjacent}} of other wrecks. An ordinary wreck takes its slots from the ship. An Interceptor takes its first slots from the wreck's own rolls, and those same rolls decide that a Sentinel wreck stands on that spot at all. So its first slot always lies in the middle row of the grid, where other slots can sit on both sides of it. How often each ship type makes a square is under [the square on a ship](/guides/ship-square).
