# Galactic Trade Terminal

**What does the Galactic Trade Terminal sell?** Crafted products and raw resources. A space station's terminal shows {{shownMin}} to {{shownMax}} items from a list of {{wares}}; any other terminal, on a planet, a freighter, or the Space Anomaly, shows {{shownMin2}} to {{shownMax2}} from a list of {{wares2}}. Part of each list is always on sale, the rest is drawn.

## On a space station

Every item a station's terminal can show, with the description the game gives it and its base value. The price at a terminal moves around that value with the system's [economy](/guides/economy). Behind the station's terminal stands the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer), who sells for Tainted Metal.

## On a planet, a freighter, or the Space Anomaly

Every terminal outside a space station, at a trading post, in a settlement, aboard a freighter, or in the Space Anomaly, keeps much the same list, and adds Starship Launch Fuel, Navigation Data, and the Exosuit Upgrade Chart. A settlement's Marketplace also has a [Settlement Technology Merchant](/guides/settlement-technology-merchant), who sells crafted components.

The pilots who land beside a terminal trade from their ships too, as [Starship Pilot](/guides/starship-pilots) lists.
