# Station technology merchants

**Where do you buy class S upgrade modules?** From the technology merchants of an inhabited [space station](/guides/space-stations): one for the starship, one for the multitool, one for the exosuit, and one for the exocraft. Each class S starship module is on sale at {{shipOne}} of stations, and the system's wealth does not move that chance. A station that sells Launch Thrusters, Pulse Engine, Hyperdrive, Starship Shield, Phase Beam, and Infra-Knife all at class S is {{sixOneIn}} stations: real, and rare.

What each module raises once installed, class by class, is on the page of its item; this page says where and how often you find it.

## Starship technology merchant

<!-- widget:starship -->

Every station sells the class C Launch Thrusters, Pulse Engine, Hyperdrive, and Starship Shield modules, and all four reactors. Every other module, at every class, is a pick of the station, and each has the same chance. There is no class roll: a Hyperdrive Module at S is its own item on the list, beside the one at B and the one at A. What each of them raises, and the figurines and drives that do not come from a merchant, are on [starship modules](/guides/starship-modules).

## Multitool technology merchant

<!-- widget:multitool -->

A class S multitool module is on sale at {{toolOne}} of stations. The limits of every Mining Beam, Boltcaster, or Scanner module are on [multitool modules](/guides/multitool-modules).

## Exosuit technology merchant

<!-- widget:exosuit -->

The exosuit merchant sells the most at S: {{suitOne}} for each module. Life Support has no class B, and the protection modules have no class C. What a Movement System or a protection module raises is on [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules).

## Exocraft technology merchant

<!-- widget:exocraft -->

The cannons, lasers, boosters, and engines of the land exocraft, and their limits, are on the pages of [Roamer](/guides/roamer-modules), [Nomad](/guides/nomad-modules), [Pilgrim](/guides/pilgrim-modules), and [Colossus](/guides/colossus-modules).

## How many at class S

<!-- widget:count -->

A starship merchant sells {{ninePerS}} modules at class S at most, one of each type. {{threeOrFour}} of stations sell three or four of them.

<!-- widget:sets -->

The four modules every ship needs, Launch Thrusters, Pulse Engine, Hyperdrive, and Starship Shield, are all at class S at {{coreOneIn}} stations.

## Why wealth does not matter

A merchant's stock has a fixed size range and draws from one list, and no part of that reads the system's wealth, economy type, conflict, or race. Wealth only sets how many of an item a merchant holds, and for an upgrade module that is one at every wealth. A wealthy system pays more class S ships and multitools, as [economy](/guides/economy) shows, but not more class S modules.

Each station keeps its stock for good. These odds hold for any station; which stations sell everything at class S is not researched yet.

## Pirate stations

A pirate station has one technology merchant, and it sells only suspicious modules: Illegal ones for the starship, the multitool, and the exosuit, nothing for the exocraft. It shows {{pirateShown}} of {{pirateAll}}: nine for the starship (Launch Thrusters, Pulse Engine, Hyperdrive, Starship Shield, and the five weapons), nine for the multitool (Mining Beam, Scanner, and the seven weapons), and four for the exosuit (Life Support, Hazard Protection, Movement, and Shield). Each is drawn with the same chance, so every one is on sale at {{pirateOne}} of pirate stations, whichever you want. There are no classes to wait for: each suspicious module is one item.

A whole set is a different matter. The four modules every ship needs are all on one pirate merchant at about one pirate station in 15, and all nine of one item almost never.

<!-- widget:pirateSets -->

An Illegal module is a gamble. A class S Mining Beam module always raises all four of its stats; an Illegal one raises one to four, but its limits reach past class S: Heat Dispersion goes to +55% against +50%. Each family's limits are on [multitool modules](/guides/multitool-modules), [starship modules](/guides/starship-modules), and [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules).

## What no merchant sells

Freighter modules never stand on a merchant's list: a derelict freighter or a pirate dreadnought gives them, and the Hive of Glass of the Swarm expedition did, as [freighter technology](/guides/freighter-technology) shows. After the shops, the next prize of a system lies on its planets, and the first thing to read is their size: [sources by planet size](/guides/sources-by-size).
