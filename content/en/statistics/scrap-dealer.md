# Scrap Dealer

**What does the Scrap Dealer sell?** Suspicious packets, repair kits, decorations, banners, and decals, and now and then a suspicious upgrade module, all for Tainted Metal: {{shownMin}} items at a time from a list of {{wares}}.

## Where to find one

The Scrap Dealer stands behind the [Galactic Trade Terminal](/guides/galactic-trade-terminal) of a space station. Beside his shop he sells the place of a derelict freighter, whose walk is the quest [Lost in Space](/quests/lost-in-space).

## What he sells

Every item he can show, with the description the game gives it and its base price in Tainted Metal; an item marked drawn is on sale at some dealers only. The contraband sells above its base price. The suspicious modules are a few of those an [Outlaw Station](/guides/outlaw-station) sells.
