# Organic frigates

**How do you get an organic frigate?** Build a Dream Aerial and call a cosmic whale with it. The first whale you meet asks you questions in a dream, and your answers pick the kind of frigate it gives.

## The Dream Aerial

An organic frigate comes from a cosmic whale that hides in a system, and a Dream Aerial calls it. Its blueprint comes from a frigate expedition: once you have finished {{expeditions}} expeditions and own {{frigates}} frigates, an expedition under way can bring it home. A Dream Aerial takes 1 Solar Mirror and 3 Living Glass.

The whale will not answer while your fleet holds {{fleet}} frigates, and you can hold one organic frigate from each system.

## The dream

The first whale you meet asks {{questions}} questions in a dream. Every answer from the second question on raises one of six counts by one, and the highest count picks your frigate's kind. Each question also offers (Wake), which ends the dream. A tie goes to Exploration first, then Speed, Support, Trade, Industrial, and Combat.

<!-- widget:dream -->

The game's table points the Speed answers at the Support count, so the Speed count never grows, and the Speed frigate looks out of reach through the dream. This may well be one of the [game bugs](/game-bugs) rather than a choice.

## Later whales

Later whales sell their frigates like any fleet. Each system has a whale of its own: everyone who calls it there meets the same creature, with the same name, kind, traits, class, and price. Its kind is one of six, Combat, Exploration, Industrial, Trade, Support, or Speed, one as likely as the next. Only the first whale's kind comes from your dream; its name is still the system's. An organic frigate never draws a bad trait, and draws only from a list of its own; how traits and classes work, and the traits of the frigates bought from fleets, is on [frigates](/guides/frigates#frigate-traits).
