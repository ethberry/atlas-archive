# Where to look

**Where in a galaxy should you look for class S items?** Anywhere you like: there is no golden ring. Tools grow all the way out: a median system at the core offers {{innerTools}} against {{outerTools}} at the rim, and {{innerUncharted}} of core systems are Uncharted, against {{outerUncharted}}. Wealth moves what you find far more than distance does, so pick a system for its [wealth](/guides/economy), not for how far out it lies.

## Rings of the galaxy

Tools grow slowly on the way out. The mean of class S stays high out to 500,000 ly because pirate systems crowd there, {{innerPirate}} against {{outerPirate}} at the rim, and a pirate class S is weak in damage, as [multitool classes](/guides/multitool-classes) explains. The median of class S climbs slowly. Wrecks stay about level.

## Stars

The stars themselves are spread evenly: a median voxel holds {{innerStars}} stars at the core and at the rim alike. What changes is how many systems hold life, not how many stars there are. There are no spiral arms either, from the core to the farthest voxels at the rim and through the whole height of the disc. Maps that draw the galaxy as a glowing spiral with arms and dark gaps between them are pretty, but the stars are not laid out that way.

So the choice comes down to one star, and [systems](/guides/systems) shows what a star can hold.
