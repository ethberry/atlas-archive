# Fossil Collector

**What does the Fossil Collector trade?** Fossils, fossil for fossil: all {{wares}} of the game, every one on sale at once. The collector of a space station asks {{barter}} times the value of the fossil you take, paid in fossils.

## Where fossils come from

A planet whose card shows Ancient Bones holds bone piles underground; digging one up gives a fossil.

## What it trades

Every fossil, with the description the game gives it and the value the trade counts for it.

<!-- widget:stock -->

## How a trade is accepted

The collector rates each fossil you offer at {{floor}} to the whole of its value, takes up to {{slots}} slots, each holding a stack, and sets the sum against {{barter}} times the value of the fossil you want. {{slots}} fossils pass at least {{threeChance}} of the time, {{fewer}} at most {{twoChance}}. The answer stays the same for the same offer until you next fly into space, so after a refusal change the offer rather than ask again.

## What fossils are for

A fossil is money or decor. Each fossil's card says {{boneValue}} units. Or build it into a skeleton: the game has {{displays}} skeleton displays for a base, each with {{displaySlots}} slots, one fossil in each, and any limb fits any limb or wing slot.

<!-- widget:displays -->

A finished skeleton can be packaged into one exhibit, which the game says can then be sold, traded, or placed again at any base. The station's other trader in kind takes corvette parts: the [Salvage Exchange](/guides/salvage-exchange).
