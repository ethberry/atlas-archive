# Settlement Technology Merchant

**What does a settlement's Technology Merchant sell?** Crafted components, from Microprocessors and Wiring Looms to Quantum Computers: {{shownMin}} items at a time from a list of {{wares}}, part of them always on sale and the rest drawn.

## Where to find one

The Technology Merchant stands in the Marketplace of a planetary settlement. On a space station the technology merchants sell upgrade modules instead: the [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants).

## What it sells

Every item it can show, with the description the game gives it and its value.
