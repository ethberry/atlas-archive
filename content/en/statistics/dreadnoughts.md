# Dreadnoughts

**How do you get a class S dreadnought?** Own a freighter first, then fight in a pirate system. Break the dreadnought's engines and it surrenders, and you can seize it. It comes at class S {{pirateS}} of the time.

<!-- widget:dreadnoughtRender -->

## When a dreadnought comes

Only once you own a freighter. When a battle is due, a pirate system always brings a dreadnought, and a third of the other systems do.

Some systems bring a dreadnought on every arrival, battle due or not: {{highArrival}} of high conflict systems and {{pirateArrival}} of pirate ones, or {{every}} of all systems. The game rolls for it with the same number every time you arrive in a system, because it draws that number from the system. So a system that brings one brings one every time, and you can farm it without waiting for the next battle.

## How the battle ends

<!-- widget:outcomes -->

You cannot hurt the dreadnought from far away: its shields are too strong. Fly in under them and shoot the hull from close up. Meanwhile it fires lasers and torpedoes at the civilian freighter. Leave the torpedoes alone: shooting them down changes nothing.

### To destroy it

Destroy it and you get a freighter module, not the ship. The best place to fight from is a trench along the deck, where the shield generators stand. There are ten, five in each of the two trenches, under the arches that span them.

<!-- widget:dreadnoughtShields -->

1. Leave the engines whole. With all four broken and the hull low, it gives up instead of dying.
2. Fly into the start of a trench. There is room inside, but steering is awkward: fly very slowly, since a little more speed makes your ship shake hard. The S key that slows you down also locks onto the nearest fighter.
3. Stay at the start of the trench and shoot along it. Break the first shield generator, then the second, then the third. When one breaks, fuel rods come up out of it: shoot those too. The far end is hard to see, so go back to the first rods once they are up, then on down the trench.
4. Clear the second trench the same way.
5. Shoot straight into the trench wall until the dreadnought goes down.

Do not fight from the engines: there you are not close enough to what you need to hit.

### To make it surrender

When it is losing, the dreadnought charges its warp: 35 seconds, and 2 more for each broken engine. It surrenders when all four engines are broken and its hull is below a quarter or all six large cannons are gone.

1. Fly in under the shields and break the four hyperdrive engines fast. They sit in a row low at the stern.

<!-- widget:dreadnoughtEngines -->

2. Fly low over the deck, still under the shields, and shoot the large cannons, the ones that fire the red laser beams. Five stand on the stepped deck at the stern, one at the bow.

<!-- widget:dreadnoughtCannons -->

3. When it surrenders, fly into its hangar, which opens on the upper deck, and choose: seize the ship, demand tribute, or leave.

## Class

There is one dreadnought, the same ship everywhere. Only its class changes, and it follows the wealth of the system as for [freighters](/guides/freighters). In a pirate system it is class S {{pirateS}} of the time, so half the players seize one at S within {{pirateHalf}} tries.

## Freighter modules

A dreadnought you destroy gives one freighter module at class S, and a system's dreadnought always gives the same one. [Freighter modules](/guides/freighter-modules) lists the seven and where players farm them.
