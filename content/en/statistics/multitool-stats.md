# Multitool stats

**What are the best stats a multitool can have?** At class S, 50% damage on a Sentinel or a staff, 50% mining on an Atlantid, and 100% scanner on an Experimental or a Royal. Every tool comes with base stats for these three, in percent. Each is rolled apart, inside a range set by the tool's [multitool type](/guides/multitool-types) and [multitool class](/guides/multitool-classes). Modules come on top, and these tables leave them out. These tables hold tools found at class S.

A class S tool can still be weak in one stat. Two tools of one type can sit far apart, and a staff's damage shows it best. [How lucky am I?](/how-lucky-am-i) places yours among them.

## How a stat is rolled

The game does not roll the stat itself. It rolls how far into the range the stat lands, the same for every type. A rifle's damage runs from 15 to 20 and a staff's from 25 to 50, so the same roll that gives a rifle 15.8 gives a staff 29.

## Damage

Sentinels and staffs shoot best: both reach 50%, and most sit close to it. Alien tools come next at 35%, then Experimental and Royal at 25%, then rifles and Atlantid at 20%. Pistols have no base damage at all.

Damage does not spread evenly. Most class S tools hold a share near 1 and the rest a share from 0.15 to 0.20, with nothing between, so the median stands above the mean. No tool here falls under that 0.15, so the lowest number is above the bottom of the range. [Multitool classes](/guides/multitool-classes) shows why.

## Mining

Atlantid tools dig best, from 35% to 50%, and even the weakest matches the top pistol and beats every other type. Pistols reach 35%, Experimental and Royal 30%, Alien 20%, and Sentinels and staffs stop at 15%. Rifles have no base mining.

## Scanner

Experimental and Royal tools scan best: every one at class S holds exactly 100%. Alien tools reach 60% and Sentinels 55%. Pistols, Atlantid, and staffs stop at 50%, and rifles scan worst, at 20%.

All of this holds for tools found at class S. Upgrade one from a lower class and the rules change: [how to upgrade a multitool](/guides/multitool-upgrades).
