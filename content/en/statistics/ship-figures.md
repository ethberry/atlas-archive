# Ship figures

**Which figure will a ship's four supercharged slots form?** Most often a corner, {{corner}}, when they are adjacent at all. [How it works](/about) explains why that matters. A ship's grid is ten slots wide and three tall, so the grid itself rules one turn out: an upright line never fits.

## By figure

Most ships land alike, because an ordinary wreck's slots come from the ship itself. Interceptors stand apart: their first slot always lies in the middle row, which favours the zigzags and the T and costs the line.

## All 19, by ship type

Figures run by how many ways they can lie: the square one way, the line two, each zigzag two, the T four, and each corner four. Inside a figure, the rarest turn comes first.
