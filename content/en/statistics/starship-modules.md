# Starship modules

**What can a starship upgrade module raise?** Each system of a starship has its own family of upgrade modules, and the class sets how many stats one install raises and how far: an S-Class Pulse Engine upgrade raises three of its stats, a C-Class one one or two. The starship merchant of a station sells them, as [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants) shows.

Each stat reads as the module's card prints it. Illegal modules are sold at an [Outlaw Station](/guides/outlaw-station) and now and then by the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer), and Rusted ones are sea salvage.

## Upgrade modules

One table per family: each class, how many stats one install raises, and the limits of each stat. Below each table a second one shows what one install rolls: how many stats it raises, how often it comes near the best its kind can add to the stat it names, and how often each stat is raised; a third shows how often a raised stat lands in the top tenth of its limits, where the Illegal modules stand out by a one-in-ten draw that raises the most stats their kind allows. Where to put them for the most damage is on [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots).

## Every starship module

The modules you build or receive, apart from upgrade modules and the cosmetics. How to upgrade the ship itself to class S is on [how to upgrade a ship](/guides/ship-upgrades). The [Base Computer Archives](/quests/base-computer-archives) of your base give the Teleport Receiver and the Efficient Thrusters, and the armourer's quest [Weapons Research](/quests/weapons-research) the Positron Ejector, the Infra-Knife Accelerator, and the Cyclotron Ballista.

### Interceptor only

A Sentinel Interceptor flies on its own core technology, and no other ship takes it; the [Interceptors](/guides/interceptors) guide says where to find one.

### Living ship only

A [living ship](/guides/living-ships) grows organs in the place of a starship's technology: none of these fits another ship, and of the starship modules it takes only the figurines, the trails, and the Atlantid Drive.

<!-- widget:onlyLiving -->

## Figurines

A figurine sits in the cockpit, takes a technology slot, and changes a stat. Six are sold for Quicksilver, the rest are expedition rewards.

<!-- widget:figurines -->

## Starship trails

A trail colours the exhaust and adds 1% to boost. It is not a drive: the Cadmium or Emeril Drive in the list of every starship module opens stars of that colour, a trail of the same name only paints the exhaust. Five trails are sold for Quicksilver, the rest are expedition rewards.

<!-- widget:trails -->

A corvette you build yourself takes these modules, and each part it is built from brings one more, as [corvettes](/guides/corvettes) shows and [corvette modules](/guides/corvette-modules) lists.
