# Salvage Exchange

**What does the Salvage Exchange trade?** Corvette parts, part for part: all {{wares}} kinds on sale at once, for {{barter}} times the value of the part you take, paid in corvette parts. {{slots}} of your parts of the same value usually buy one of its.

## Where to find one

The Salvage Exchange is kept by the attendant of a space station's corvette workshop, who also sells a basic set of parts for units. What a first build needs is under [corvettes](/guides/corvettes).

## What it trades

Every part it can show, with the description the game gives it and the value the trade counts for it.

## What it takes

The parts it accepts in trade are not the same list as the parts it sells: it takes some it never sells, the [Forsaken Trader](/guides/forsaken-trader)'s among them, and leaves out some it does.

<!-- widget:wanted -->

## How a trade is accepted

The exchange rates each part you offer at {{floor}} to the whole of its value, takes up to {{slots}} slots, each holding a stack, and sets the sum against {{barter}} times the value of the part you want. The nearer it comes, the likelier a yes: {{slots}} parts of the same value pass at least {{threeChance}} of the time, {{fewer}} at most {{twoChance}}. The answer stays the same for the same offer until you next fly into space, so after a refusal change the offer rather than ask again.

## What it sells for units

The same attendant's second option, Purchase basic modules, sells {{wares2}} plain parts for units, all on sale at once.
