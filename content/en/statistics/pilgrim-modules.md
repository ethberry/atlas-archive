# Pilgrim modules

**What can the Pilgrim carry?** The Pilgrim, the motorbike, takes every land exocraft module, 26 technology slots of them, and upgrade modules of four kinds from C to S: Mounted Cannon, Exocraft Mining Laser, Exocraft Boosters, and Fusion Engine.

The Roamer, Nomad, Pilgrim, and Colossus share their upgrade modules and most of their technology; what sets them apart is the room. Each stat reads as the module's card prints it.

## Every Pilgrim module

The quest [Exocraft Technician](/quests/exocraft-technician) gives the blueprints of the Exocraft Radar and its two upgrades, the Mounted Cannon, the Exocraft Boosters, and both mining lasers.

## How much fits

With every module on it the Pilgrim has 8 slots left for upgrade modules, not the twelve that three of each kind would take. Pick two kinds to fill and give the third two. Every core technology takes three upgrade modules at most, and a fourth of the same kind switches all of them off. Where to put them for the most effect is on [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots), and where to buy them on [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants).

## Supercharged slots

The Pilgrim has three supercharged slots, and they stand in the same places for every player: slot 4 of the top row, slot 10 of the middle row, slot 2 of the bottom row, counting from the left. There is nothing to roll. None of the three is adjacent to another, so each can lift one kind: give them to the Fusion Engine, the Exocraft Boosters, and the Exocraft Mining Laser, one module of each, and set the rest of that kind and its own technology around it, adjacent.

## Upgrade modules

One table per family: each class, how many stats one install raises, and the limits of each stat. Below each table a second one shows what one install rolls: how many stats it raises, how often it comes near the best its kind can add to the stat it names, and how often each stat is raised; a third shows how often a raised stat lands in the top tenth of its limits.
