# Ship stats

**How strong can a claimed ship be?** It depends on the ship type: a Fighter reaches {{damageFighterHigh}} damage, a Hauler {{shieldHaulerHigh}} shield, and an Exotic {{agileExoticHigh}} maneuverability, while an Interceptor reads {{hyperdriveInterceptorHigh}} hyperdrive. Every ship comes with base stats for these four, in percent. Each is rolled apart, inside a range set by its [ship type](/guides/ship-types) and [ship class](/guides/ship-classes). The tables below show what reaches your hangar, as the number on a bare card: no module installed, and the starter cannon left out. [How lucky am I?](/how-lucky-am-i) places a ship you claimed among them.

## How a stat is rolled

A stat is rolled as on [multitool stats](/guides/multitool-stats): somewhere inside the range of its type and class.

A ship rolls twice. The wreck shows one roll. When you claim it, the ship takes a second roll and holds the better of the two, stat by stat. So a claimed ship never reads below its wreck, and often reads above it.

The second roll comes with the ship's name. Wrecks of one type in a system that carry one name hold one second roll, and differ only in the wreck's own roll. A system holds only a few names per type, and one for Exotics. Interceptors work the other way: each has a name of its own, and every Interceptor of a system shares one second roll.

## Reading the card

The tables below are the numbers on a ship's card, read while you fly that ship: the card moves with the ship you are flying, damage by up to 4 and maneuverability by up to 57.5. A working starter cannon adds 20 to 41 damage on top of them.

## Damage

Fighters shoot best: up to {{damageFighterHigh}}, with a median of {{damageFighterMedian}}. Interceptors follow at {{damageInterceptorMedian}}, then Exotics at {{damageExoticMedian}} and Solars at {{damageSolarMedian}}. Shuttles stop at {{damageShuttleHigh}} and Haulers come last, with a median of {{damageHaulerMedian}}. Explorers have no base damage.

The wreck's roll for damage is the one that set its class, as on [ship classes](/guides/ship-classes). Outside pirate systems class S sits at the top of that roll, so every Exotic and Shuttle holds nearly its top damage. In a pirate system class S sits at 0.15 to 0.20, so a Hauler or Fighter from one needs the second roll to do better.

## Shield

Haulers hold the most: from {{shieldHaulerLow}} to {{shieldHaulerHigh}}. Exotics come next at {{shieldExoticMedian}}, and every other type sits near 200.

The wreck's roll for shield barely moves: a share near 0.20 or 0.32, and near 0.52 on an Interceptor. Only the second roll lifts it, so no ship reads near the bottom of its range, and more than half of Interceptors sit exactly on that floor.

## Hyperdrive

Interceptors read far above the rest, from {{hyperdriveInterceptorLow}} to {{hyperdriveInterceptorHigh}}: their stat is the smallest, but it works on 600 where other types have 100. Among the rest Explorers lead at {{hyperdriveExplorerMedian}}, then Exotics at {{hyperdriveExoticMedian}}, Haulers at {{hyperdriveHaulerMedian}}, Solars at {{hyperdriveSolarMedian}}, and Shuttles at {{hyperdriveShuttleMedian}}. Fighters have no base hyperdrive.

<!-- widget:hyperdrive -->

## Maneuverability

Exotics turn best, with a median of {{agileExoticMedian}} and a top of {{agileExoticHigh}}. Fighters follow at {{agileFighterMedian}} and Solars at {{agileSolarMedian}}. Explorers and Interceptors sit near {{agileInterceptorMedian}}, Shuttles at {{agileShuttleMedian}}, and Haulers last at {{agileHaulerMedian}}.

## Found and upgraded

These tables hold ships found at class S. A ship found lower and upgraded at a station is not in them: what its stats do on the way up is not measured yet.

What an upgrade does give you is under [how to upgrade a ship](/guides/ship-upgrades).
