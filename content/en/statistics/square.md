# The square on a multitool

**How rare is a square of supercharged slots on a multitool?** About one tool in a thousand at best. {{best}} tools land on a square most often: {{bestOneIn}}. {{worst}} tools are the worst bet at {{worstOneIn}}. Source hardly matters, and once the slots are adjacent, neither does type: every type makes a square 6% to 8% of the time. What sets a rare type apart is how seldom its slots are adjacent at all, and [How lucky am I?](/how-lucky-am-i) scores a square as rarer still than adjacent slots.

A square fits {{places}} places on a multitool, and a ship has odds of its own: [the square on a ship](/guides/ship-square). What a square adds to damage, against every other figure, is on [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots). {{listShare}} of systems list one, and {{listNoStaff}} list one that is not a staff.

Each tool is counted as you find it, in [Euclid](/galaxy/b7e59a53-13de-521b-af6b-dcbf6280eb9d).

### Multitools

### Staffs

For [staffs](/guides/staffs) class is the whole story: no class C staff is a square, class B gives {{staffB}}, and class A {{staffA}}.

## What we list

Tools we keep for a system, one of each type and figure, as [how it works](/about) describes it, before the showcase leaves out every square. The rare types are favoured, so these are not the shares of a whole galaxy.
