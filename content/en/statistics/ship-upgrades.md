# How to upgrade a ship

**Should you upgrade a ship or find one at S?** Upgrade for the slots, and find one at S for the stats. The slots an upgrade gives are known before you pay. What it does to a ship's stats is not measured yet.

## Where to upgrade

Any inhabited or abandoned [space station](/guides/space-stations) will do. An abandoned station sells no technology, but it still offers the upgrade.

## What an upgrade changes

The class goes up and the supercharged slots fill towards four. The slots a ship holds stay where they are, and each step adds the next slot from a list that belongs to the ship. That list is fixed, so upgrading from C or from B ends on one result, and you cannot get another by paying again.

<!-- widget:slots -->

An ordinary ship draws all its slots from one list, so every class of one ship ends on the same four. That is why the class changes nothing here: {{ordinary}} are adjacent at every class. An Interceptor takes its first slots from the wreck and the rest from a list of its system, so two wrecks of one system can end apart. It reads {{interceptorC}} at class C and {{interceptorS}} at class S.

## Stats after an upgrade

Not measured yet. [Ship stats](/guides/ship-stats) holds ships found at class S and nothing else, so its numbers say nothing about an upgraded ship. A multitool's upgrade is measured, as [how to upgrade a multitool](/guides/multitool-upgrades) shows, but a ship's stats come from two rolls, so that rule does not tell us what happens here.

So if the stats are what you want, find the ship at class S. If the slots are what you want, any class will do. Modules add on top of either, and [station upgrade modules](/guides/station-upgrades) says where class S ones are sold.

An upgrade fills the empty supercharged slots, and you cannot choose where they land. [Ship figures](/guides/ship-figures) shows where they fall.
