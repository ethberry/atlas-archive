# Living ship finder

**What shapes can a living ship have?** This living ship builder puts one together from its parts. Every shape here is one the game makes, but no system can be named for it. A living ship is always class S, so the shape is the one thing that differs between them.

<!-- widget:finder -->

## How to use it

Pick the head first. Click a part of the ship, or a name under it, to see its choices; a part you leave open can be anything. A choice the game never makes together with your other picks is greyed out. The ship is drawn in plain grey, because the colours are not part of the shape.

A living ship has one body. On it sit a head, round, jaw, pointed, or flat, and a flat head can carry a fin; a ridge along the back or none; feelers or none; one of five undersides; a bell or a cone for the engine; and short wings, long wings, or none. Two undersides bring more choices: the small fins can add rear pedals, and the open underside a belly and pedals.

## Getting a living ship

The crash site where the ship waits fixes its shape: the same site always holds the same living ship. The Void Egg's quest picks that site for you, at random among the crash sites of the planet it sends you to, so no place can be named for a shape. From the second ship on you can turn down the ship you are offered: the egg comes back, and the next ship waits at a new site. [Living ships](/guides/living-ships) explains the quest and what each more ship costs.

An organic frigate comes another way: you call a cosmic whale with a Dream Aerial, and [organic frigates](/guides/organic-frigates) explains how. A crashed Sentinel ship is one whose system can be named by its shape, and the [Interceptor finder](/interceptor-finder) gives that system.
