# Capital freighter finder

**Where do you find a capital freighter with the shape you want?** In a system that saves that shape, because a system's capital freighter is built the same way on every visit. Pick the parts you want, sign in, and you get the portal glyphs of one system in [Euclid](/galaxy/b7e59a53-13de-521b-af6b-dcbf6280eb9d).

## How to use it

Pick the hull first. Click a part of the ship, or a name under it, to see its choices; a part you leave open can be anything. A choice that no system holds together with your other picks is greyed out. The ship is drawn in plain grey, because the colours are not part of the search.

A capital freighter has a wedge hull, which carries a spine of cargo modules with a tower or a block of thrusters on top, or a block hull, a long box of cargo sections with a bridge on top. The length also sets the size: a short capital freighter is a medium freighter, and a medium or long one is a large freighter.

## Getting a capital freighter

A capital freighter comes from a space battle. A battle comes when you warp in with one due, once 3 hours of play and 5 warps have passed since the last. Your first two battles save a regular freighter; from the third on, the freighter you save is the capital one of the system. While you own no freighter, its captain gives it to you for free. Save the game in a neighbouring system with the battle due, warp in, and load again until the ship comes at the class you want.

A pirate system gives class S most often, as [freighters](/guides/freighters) shows. The finder still answers with a wealthy system, because it searches by shape, and once you own a freighter a pirate system always sends a [dreadnought](/guides/dreadnoughts) instead of the ship you picked. A wealthy system can bring one too, so try it before you own a freighter. A dreadnought you destroy pays [freighter modules](/guides/freighter-modules) for the freighter you end up with.

## What stays the same in a system

The shape and the size are the same on every visit. The class is not: it is rolled again on every try, so the finder gives you the shape, not the class. The [freighter finder](/freighter-finder) shows the shapes of regular freighters, and the [Interceptor finder](/interceptor-finder) gives a system by the shape of its crashed Sentinel ship.
